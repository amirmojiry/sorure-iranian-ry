A group of Iranians, coordinated by **Sorure Iranian ry**, participated in a volunteer initiative to remove invasive plants in the city of Vaasa. Together, they contributed **approximately 250 person-hours of volunteer work**, clearing a large area.

At the end of the initiative, the volunteers were thanked at a small appreciation ceremony. A modest amount paid by the City of Vaasa to the association was distributed among the volunteers in the form of shopping gift cards.

We hope to continue participating in volunteer activities that benefit the city of Vaasa in the future.
