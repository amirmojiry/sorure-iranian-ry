Sorure Iranian ry has compiled a searchable index of **315 Persian and related catalogue records** from [Vaasa City Library’s Finna catalogue](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/): **311 items** classified under Persian (`fas`) and **four music CDs** classified under the broader label Iranian languages (`ira`). It covers printed books, audiobooks, music, audio recordings and DVDs. Click any catalogue title to open its Finna record.

**The index is based on catalogue exports collected on 19–20 November 2026, not a live check of physical copies, library branches, lending status or reservations.** A catalogue hit alone does not prove that a physical copy is currently held by a Vaasa branch.

Publication years appear in **293 records** and ISBNs in **172**. Cover-image URL metadata supplies creator names for **292 records**, with editorial Persian-script renderings provided. All **315 records** now have titles in Persian or Arabic script; one title is originally in Arabic, and some English titles have approximate editorial translations.

## Search and sort

Search by original or Persian title, original or Persian creator name, year or ISBN. **Click any column heading to sort ascending; click again to reverse the order.** You can also filter by material type, the original **Finna language label** (`fas` or `ira`) and **library location** (Main library, Huudi, Variska or Unknown). On mobile, scroll horizontally to see all columns.

## How to interpret the fields

- **Catalogue title:** The original entry from the library feed; links to its specific record in Finna.
- **Title in Persian or Arabic script:** Most are editorial transcriptions or translations, sometimes approximate and not independently verified against physical covers. The Arabic title “أجراس ناعمة / شظايا من يوميات الغراب” was supplied verbatim.
- **Creator:** Taken from catalogue cover-image URL metadata. The Persian version is an editorial rendering, not an independently verified name. For nonbooks, the person or organization may be an artist, performer or film director rather than an author.
- **Material type:** Based on the format reported by Finna; it is not a claim that a copy is available for loan.
- **Library location:** Based on the supplied filtered exports and specific branch assignments; it is not live lending availability. “Unknown” means no location was established in these sources.
- **Finna language label:** The original 311 records have `fas` (Persian); the four added CDs have `ira` (Iranian languages). This is a broader linguistic classification, not a synonym for Persian.
- **Publication year:** Preserved as supplied. Some years are Solar Hijri and others Gregorian; numeric sorting does not convert between calendars.
- **RSS record date:** Derived from the feed's publication-date field; this **does not establish the date the item was purchased, acquired, or shelved**.

Some records may be tagged with Persian as one of their languages without consisting entirely of Persian-language content. Open the [Finna catalogue](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/) record to check branch holdings and loan status.
