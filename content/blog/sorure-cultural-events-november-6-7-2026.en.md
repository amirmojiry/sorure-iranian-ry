**Sorure Iranian ry** will participate in two cultural events in Vaasa on **Friday 6 and Saturday 7 November 2026**. We warmly invite everyone interested in cultural exchange to meet the association and discover aspects of Iranian culture.

## Friday 6 November | Identity fashion event

The association will take part in the **Identity** fashion event, participating in the fashion show and hosting a **food table**. The event brings together fashion, beauty and cultural diversity.

**Time:** Friday 6 November 2026, 19:00 (doors open at 18:30)

**Venue:** Campus Alere, Wolffintie 31, Vaasa

**Admission:** €5; tickets are purchased at the venue.

{{< business-link href="https://www.instagram.com/identity.fi" label="Identity event information" >}}

## Saturday 7 November | World’s Day (Maailman päivä)

Sorure Iranian ry will also participate in **World’s Day** at the **Alma Adult Education Centre**. Our contribution will include **Iranian music and Kurdish dance performances**, as well as an **Iran table**.

At the Iran table, visitors can find **Iranian food for sale, artworks, traditional Hafez poetry divination and books about Iran**.

**Time:** Saturday 7 November 2026, 12:00–15:00

**Venue:** Alma, Raastuvankatu 31, Vaasa

**Admission:** Free

{{< business-link href="https://www.mirahouse.fi/maailman-paiva-world-s-day-varldens-dag" label="World’s Day event details" >}}

We look forward to welcoming visitors to both events and sharing opportunities for conversation, cultural discovery and community connection.
