Finnish and Swedish dominate the [Vaasa City Library catalogue in Finna](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/), but the language filter also highlights a wide range of other languages. Persian occupies a notable position in that list.

In the **19 November 2026 snapshot of language-filter counts supplied for this analysis**, **Persian has 311 catalogue results**, placing it **18th among all 146 displayed categories**. Excluding the two labels that do not name a specific language—**“No linguistic content, Not applicable”** and **“Multiple languages”**—places Persian **16th among 144 remaining categories**.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---:|
| All categories listed | **146** |
| Categories excluding the two non-specific labels | **144** |
| Persian-tagged catalogue results | **311** |
| Persian rank across all categories | **18** |
| Persian rank excluding the two non-specific labels | **16** |

A “record” here means a catalogue search result, which may not be a printed book (it could be a music album, a film, etc.). The counts come from the “Language” filter.

## Persian among the catalogue's other languages

Finnish leads with **155,018** results, followed by Swedish (**111,302**) and English (**50,084**). Much farther down the list, the counts are much closer together: **Japanese has 316**, **Estonian 313**, and **Persian 311**. Persian is therefore just **two records behind Estonian** and **five behind Japanese**, while appearing ahead of **Ukrainian (256)**, **Turkish (184)** and **Kurdish (125)**.

## Languages also spoken in Iran

Iran is multilingual. Persian, Azerbaijani, Kurdish, Arabic, Armenian and several other languages are spoken in different parts of the country. **Their speakers and cultural histories are not confined to Iran**: Arabic is used across many countries, Kurdish in several Middle Eastern countries, Armenian in Armenia and diasporic communities, and Turkic languages across a much wider region.

The following catalogue labels are also associated with languages spoken in Iran:

**Turkish and Azerbaijani are distinct catalogue labels**. **Dari** is listed separately from Persian, while **Iranian languages** is an umbrella linguistic category.

## Four related music CDs hidden under “Iranian languages”

A separate review of [Finna's Iranian languages (`ira`) category](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/Search/Results?filter%5B%5D=%7Elanguage%3A%22ira%22&type=AllFields&lng=en-gb) identified **four audio/music CDs** absent from the 311 records assigned to the Persian (`fas`) label. Their artists and titles connect them with Persian or Iranian music:

| Editorial title in Persian | Artist | Finna record |
|---|---|---|
| آریان ۲: و اما عشق | Arian Band | [ARIAN II VA AMMA ESHGH](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/Record/vaasa.09936617-2576-4fcd-ac36-0d6f0af412c8) |
| دو چهره | Behnam | [DOUBLE FACED](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/Record/vaasa.05139018-c85a-400e-bd79-1e107ddc18d0) |
| برای تو | Mohammad Khakpour | [TO YOU](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/Record/vaasa.42a54432-764c-4d3c-bb76-e88b7ffb0548) |
| چه شورها دارم، غم جانکاهی | Gholamhosein Banan | [CHE SHUURHA DARÄM GHÄME DJANKAHI](https://vaasankirjasto.finna.fi/Record/vaasa.f14f3c05-62b0-4067-a4b7-409417f424e9) |

Sorure Iranian ry has therefore added them to its [expanded searchable index of **315 Persian and related records**]({{< relref "blog/persian-books-vaasa-library-2026" >}}): **311 under Persian + four under Iranian languages**. None was duplicated in the original list.

**The distinction matters for the ranking:** the official Finna Persian facet remains **311** (rank **18** in the original list), and the separate Iranian languages facet remains **4**. The adjusted **315** would fall between Japanese (316) and Estonian (313) in a hypothetical editorial comparison, **but it is not the official Persian rank or count**. Nor can a CD's recorded language be established from its format alone; not every track is necessarily sung in Persian.

This example illustrates why searching only one language filter may overlook culturally relevant materials catalogued under a broader label.
