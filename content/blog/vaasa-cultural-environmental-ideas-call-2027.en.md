**Have an idea for an art exhibition, community workshop, cultural event or environmental initiative in Vaasa?** Sorure Iranian ry invites residents and community members to share ideas that could be developed and carried out together through the association in 2027.

The City of Vaasa currently has two funding opportunities. You do **not** need to prepare a formal grant application to suggest an idea to us.

## 1. Eco Bonus — funding for environmental action

- **Funding:** €100–€1,000 per supported proposal.
- **City application deadline:** **22 November 2026** (application period: 3–22 November).
- **Implementation period:** **1 January–28 February 2027**.
- **Suitable ideas:** reducing food waste, reuse and recycling, energy efficiency, biodiversity, sustainable consumption, community clean-ups, environmental workshops and small practical investments.

The Eco Bonus is intended for associations and resident communities operating in Vaasa. Projects should create environmental benefits and opportunities for residents to participate. Funding is subject to the City’s conditions and approval.

[Official Eco Bonus information and application details](https://www.vaasa.fi/en/about-vaasa-and-the-vaasa-region/evolving-vaasa/carbon-neutral-vaasa-202x/the-citys-climate-efforts/vaasa-a-green-leaf-city/eco-bonus-for-climate-action/) · [Latest City announcement (3 November 2026)](https://www.vaasa.fi/en/news/third-eco-bonus-application-round-concludes-vaasas-green-leaf-year/)

## 2. Cultural grants for 2027

- **Project grant:** generally **€300–€2,500**. Separate operating grants are also available for eligible associations.
- **City application deadline:** **30 November 2026** (application period: 1–30 November).
- **Implementation period:** **during 2027**.
- **Suitable ideas:** art exhibitions, music, literature, handicrafts, public workshops, cultural events, intercultural projects and activities that strengthen community participation.

Cultural projects should take place in Vaasa and be accessible to the public. The City also accepts applications from individuals and working groups, but this call invites proposals to be considered **for implementation through Sorure Iranian ry**.

[City of Vaasa's 2027 cultural grant announcement](https://www.vaasa.fi/en/news/applications-for-cultural-grants-for-2027-will-open-in-november/) · [Online grant application system (WebTimmi)](https://varaus.vaasa.fi/WebTimmi/#/26)

**Important for direct applicants:** The City instructs applicants to select **2026** in the online system, even though the grant concerns activities in 2027.

## How to suggest an idea

Please email **[sorureiranianry@gmail.com](mailto:sorureiranianry@gmail.com)** with a short description. It is enough to tell us:

1. What would you like to organise or build?
2. Who could participate, and how would the public benefit?
3. Where and approximately when could it happen?
4. What is your rough budget, and what help or partners would you need?

**Suggested dates for sending ideas to the association:**

- **Eco Bonus ideas: by 19 November 2026.**
- **Cultural ideas: by 23 November 2026.**

These are **Sorure Iranian ry's preferred internal dates**, not the City's official deadlines. Early messages help us review feasibility, coordinate volunteers and prepare applications on time.

Ideas are welcome in **Persian, Finnish or English**. The association will review proposals based on suitability, resources and the relevant grant rules. Sending an idea does not guarantee that an application will be submitted or funding awarded.

Let's create inclusive, creative and more sustainable activities in Vaasa together.
