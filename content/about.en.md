Sorure Iranian ry is a registered association based in Vaasa, Finland. Since 2023, we have brought people together around Iranian cultural heritage, community traditions and everyday opportunities to meet and learn.

Our activities include public cultural events, literary and artistic encounters, Finnish-language learning, activities for children and families, and volunteering. We welcome cultural exchange and cooperation with other communities and organisations in Vaasa.
