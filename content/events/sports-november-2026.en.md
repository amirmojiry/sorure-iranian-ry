On Friday, 13 November 2026, we met at Hietalahden koulu in Vaasa for a friendly evening of sports. Thirteen people took part: nine men, three women and one child.

We played volleyball from 19:00 to 20:30, followed by approximately 20 minutes of futsal with small goals. The women also played badminton. The gathering continued until 21:00.

Thank you to everyone who joined us. We look forward to more sports evenings together!
